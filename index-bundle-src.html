I'm passionate about sports, media, and culture and I've been building real skills across all three. I've worked on content strategy and athlete branding at THE·TEAM, helping previous NBA players expand their reach in international markets through highlight reels, player bio decks, and targeted content built for global audiences. I've also run A/B tested email campaigns and global PR outreach to support international distribution. On top of that, I founded a youth basketball organization focused on giving underserved kids access to the sport, running community events, raising funds, and building the brand from the ground up. I'm drawn to the creative side of connecting people to things they care about, whether that's telling an athlete's story or finding the right angle to break into a new market, and I want to work on projects that actually move people.
